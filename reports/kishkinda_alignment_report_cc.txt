Kishkindha alignment pilot — results
I built align_recension.py (one script, parameterised by kanda — python3 align_recension.py N) and ran it over Kishkindha. It emitted 66 map_4_{sarga}.json files and alignment_report_kishkindha.md. Nothing was written to data.json or the .txt files.

Method note: raw similarity looked alarming at first (31/67 below 0.85) but that was two artifacts, both handled: SequenceMatcher's default autojunk corrupts Devanagari ratios (I use autojunk=False), and data.json stores AshuVj's combined verse blocks redundantly on every shloka number they span (403 rows across 52 sargas). I reconstruct true per-shloka Sanskrit by splitting each shloka_text on its embedded 4.s.n stamps — this restored clean alignments (e.g. sarga 29: 0.50 → 0.95) and gives shloka-level mapping precision.

Things the pilot turned up that go beyond the brief
Sarga 16 FAILED (sim 0.11) — a source defect in the .txt. कks1-67.txt's sarga 16 (षोडशः सर्गः (सर्ग 16)) contains the hemanta-ṛtu / Godavari passage — Aranya-kanda material, not the real Kishkindha 16 (Vali rebuking Tara, which is in data.json). The sarga number is present, so the numeric integrity check passes; only content alignment caught it. No map was emitted. This needs a source fix, not an alignment decision.
Two cross-sarga boundary shifts (recensions disagree on where sargas end, not just internal division): GP 52's tail (v20–32) = data.json 53's head (v1–13) at ratio 0.90, and GP 56's tail (v20–24) = data.json 57's head (v1–7) at 0.62. Reported in a "read first" section 0.
2 suspect joins (sargas 5, 17) where a different data.json verse matches clearly better — flagged, not auto-rerouted.
